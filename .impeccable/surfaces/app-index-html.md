# Surface: Audiolivros PWA (all screens)

Scope: the whole iPhone PWA — unlock, Biblioteca, Ouvindo, Capítulos, Texto, Marcadores. Mode: Operate.
Audience/job: the owner, commuting or driving; resume in one tap, steer from lock screen, glance for "where am I / how long to the next part".

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each book is a metro line — chapters are stations, parts are interchanges, the listener is the train. Refuses the cover-art-and-centered-controls player.

OWN-WORLD: Midnight-blue enamel ground (porcelain-tile in light mode), porcelain-white condensed station type (Barlow Condensed) over Barlow body, one line ink per book (scarlet, cobalt, amber, green), crisp 1.5px porcelain outlines, white station dots and interchange rings, departure-board rows with tabular minutes.

STORY: Open → see your train on the line, the station you are at, and minutes to the next stops → tap play or a stop → lock the phone.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Ouvindo. Part badge pill in line ink; chapter name huge in condensed caps; current section beneath; a "Próximas paradas" board listing the next sections with minutes-away; the chapter drawn as a thick line with section ticks and the train marker = the scrubber; big porcelain play ring centered below with skip/chapter controls; speed · sleep · mark row; tab bar.

FORM: Linha do Metrô (challenger "midnight transit diagram" fused with São Paulo Metrô signage); user-chosen over the roll; seed key 76e25e77. Signature interaction: drag the train along the line; the board names the stop under your finger.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
